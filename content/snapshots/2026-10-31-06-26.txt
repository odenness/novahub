# IDEAL STAT LINES
Every piece of gear has “Stat Lines” that comes on it. These Stat Lines can be changed by using “Synch Stones” or other pieces of Gear of the equivalent grade (Yellow/Unique Synch Stones for Yellow/Unique gear). If you use lower grade Synch Stones (Yellow/Unique Synch Stone for an Orange/Heroic piece of gear, you will have a 10% chance for the stat to get rerolled - never do this).
Synch Stones are obtained via Substance Morphing either Synch Stone Fragments (mainly obtained by running dungeons) or any Yellow/Unique Gear piece.
Another method of rerolling “Stat Lines” is by using orange “Soul Codex: Reset” books (referred to as “Reset Books”). These reroll every single Stat Line at once.
When rolling for lines on gear, always prioritize the lowest % roll lines (≈ x% listed below). It can be a nightmare fishing for the lower % lines especially on Heroic gear. If you get the BIS combination, you can transfer your piece of gear into another using “Transfer Stones” to preserve your Stat Lines. Try to avoid getting a low % line on the extra Philosopher Stone line because that line will not carry over and you’ll have to fish for the line again.
Be careful not to waste Orange/Heroic transfer Stones as these are much harder to come by. Transferring Yellow/Unique Gear to another Yellow/Unique Gear piece is pretty easy but put thought into transferring anything to Orange/Heroic gear. (Check Chapter 5 - Transfer)

Ex: Weapon Damage Boost > Damage Boost in terms of actual damage value but Damage Boost will be listed higher on the list below because it has a lower % chance to roll). Percentages on each state vary from piece to piece but the relative difference is the same, you can use this as the benchmark to identify rarity amongst the Stat Lines.

# SKILLS
There are 3 different types of Skills: Active, Passive, and Stigma
All skills have skill levels with Active Skills and Stigma Skills unlocking extra Specialty Perks when reaching a certain level (listed under the “Specialty” section in the image below). We’ll refer to these as "Specialty Perks” from now on.
Active Skills
Active Skills can be leveled to 10 using regular skill points called Wisdom Stones that you obtain by leveling, turning in Feathers (world completion stuff), and Nightmare + Shugo Festival Shops.
You can get additional levels on skills via gear Stat Lines, Daevanion Board, and Arcana. You can select up to 3 Specialty Perks that are selectable only when reaching a certain level indicated by the number on the icon. You will have to equip the Specialty Perk into an open slot. Level 8 unlocks the first 3 options and 1 slot. Level 12 unlocks the 4th Specialty Perk and an additional slot allowing you to equip 2 Specialty Perks. Level 16 unlocks the final Specialty Perk. Level 20 unlocks the 3rd slot.
Every class has key Level 20 Active Skills that are a must for the build to truly come online (will try and add a written Class Guide section to the doc). Make it an early priority to unlock these, some classes such as Gladiator (Overhead Slam) and Templar (Judgment) have over 60% of their damage coming from just a single skill.
Depending on the class, you’ll want 2-5 skills at level 20 at the endgame in current KR (start of Chapter 1, lvl 50 expansion). Global launch will likely have less Arcana cards available making it more difficult to level skills. Lock in on 2 of your most important skills early on.
In order to hit level 20 on your Active Skills you’ll need to prioritize getting them on your Ring Stat Lines and your Arcana (see Chapter 6 - Arcana section below).
Passive Skills
Passive Skills are exactly what you’d assume them to be. These can be upgraded the same way Active Skills are upgraded. Passives do not have Specialty Perks.
Once you level 20 your main Active Skills, focus on your key Passive Skills (every class has them, will try and add this in a future Class Guide section). There is a lot of damage coming from these passives, current end-game KR players push some to the level 25-30 range
Stigma Skills
Stigma Skills are “special skills” that generally have bigger cooldowns. These skills have 5 available Perks unlocked by skill levels and you can equip up to 6 (there’s a chance this could be lower for global though).
With Stigma Skills, you get the benefit of all Perks as long as you unlock them with levels. Stigma Skills use Stigma Shards to level and can be leveled up to 20 for the final Perk. After the Chapter 1, level 50 update, you can level Stigma Skills to 25 with the last 5 levels coming from golden Superior Stigma Shards.
Stigma Shards are obtained by combining “Unstable Stigma Shards” that you’ll get from a variety of PVE content or directly from Abyss Points, a currency in Aion 2. Abyss Points are mainly obtained from Supply Request, Daily Duties, Weekly Commands (Contract-like quests), and farming players or mobs in the Abyss.
Superior Stigma Shards are obtained when leveling from 45 to 50, and additional Superior Stigma Shards can be obtained from getting other Stigma skills to lvl 20. You get 1 Superior Stigma Shard for every lvl 20 Stigma skill. When everything is maxed, you can have 3 skills at lvl 25, 1 skill at lvl 23, and the rest at lvl 20.
Key Stigma Skills offer a lot of power when leveled. Generally these Stigma Skills offer major buffs and increase key Passive Skills amplifying both Damage and Support. Supports especially can be gatekept by lacking specific Stigma Skill levels (IE: Undefeated Mantra, Light of Protection) as they significantly boost group performance.

# ENHANCEMENT (UPGRADING YOUR GEAR)
A - GROWTH
This is your general Gear enhancing system consistent with just about every Korean MMO where you simply spend Kinah and Enhance Stones or Amplify Stones to + up your gear.
Enhance Stones are obtained through a lot of different sources and content. These are used until +15 or +20
Amplify Stones are obtained by Substance Morphing Amplify Fragments with Enhance Stones.
Yellow/Unique Gear can go to +15 then has 5 Amp levels. Orange/Heroic Gear can go to +20 then has 5 amp levels. The % for success “soft resets” back to 60% from +15 to +16.
Additional Notes: Enhance Stones bottleneck for players who swipe
Potential Enhance
Update: Potential is a PVP exclusive stat in Global, you can disregard this for PVE. The explanation below is how it works on the Asia server.
Potential Enhance is a system that adds PVE Damage Boost/Tolerance on gear. Crafted gear automatically gets max Potential Stats and nearly double the value compared to Dungeon gear only on the Asian servers, this is not the case on the Global servers. It is not confirmed whether or not Crafted Potential Stats are higher than Dungeon Gear on Global yet. This is explained in Further Detail here
In order to Potential Enhance your gear, you’ll need Potential Enhancement Material. This is obtained by Substance Morphing gear into Potential Stones. Potential Stones have Tiers and the Item Level of the gear you Substance Morph will determine the Tier. Currently Potential Stones go from 1* to 5* then to 1* “prestiged”
Aside from making this overly complicated, the system is pretty simple - you can convert extra gear into Potential Stones of the equal tier and use it to increase your Damage Boost/Tolerance on an exclusive “Potential” line
B - MANASTONE/SOULSTONE LINES
Manastones/Soulstones are items that you’ll use on your gear for RNG stats. Manastones are for Weapons and Armor, Soulstones are for Accessories. Every time you consume a single stone, you’ll roll random lines on all your Manastone/Soulstone sockets, in the case of the image below, 5 lines. You’ll be wanting to roll Superiors on end-game gear. Do not waste too many of these, if at all, on gear you’ll replace soon.
Try to roll at least 1 yellow Damage Boost stat from below. There is a big jump from blue (30) to yellow Boost (100). 100 Boost = 1% of that stat on your stat sheet, IE: Gold Weapon Damage Boost +100 = 1% Weapon Damage Boost.
Having 2 yellow/unique Attack lines is similar to 1 yellow/unique Damage Boost line
A single yellow/unique Attack (10) line is a decent placeholder roll if you are low on Stones.
Order from best to worst:
Front/Back Damage Boost > Weapon Damage Boost ≈ Critical Damage Boost > Damage Boost > Attack
Manastones/Soulstones do not carry over when you transfer/upgrade gear so don’t go all-in on gear that’s replaced. Superior Mana/Soulstones get very expensive.
C - THEOSTONES
Theostones are items you socket into your weapons. You can socket 1 per item and the same Theostones do not stack. Theostones have a % chance to proc on hit with effects ranging from CC to Damage. General rule of thumb, the higher the Combat Speed netting you more hits, the more value lower % Theostones become. Keep in mind ping impacts this.
Theostones make up for very little overall DPS, even with yellow (highest grade) Theostones. Until this get’s buffed, it’s insignificant in PVE…some people actually just take the coolest looking Theostone considering Theostones give a glow effect on your weapon. The glow below is from the Earth Theostone.
D - SOUL BINDING
Soul Binding is a very simple enhancing system that is unnecessarily confusing at first because of the names. There are 3 different types of Soul Binding - Bind, Sync, and Reset. Soul Binding is relative to your Stat Lines.
Bind
“Bind” increases the blue Soul Binding Rate bar that increases the effectiveness of each Stat Line indicated by the yellow bar. The Blue Soul Binding Rate bar will be 100% when all the yellow lines are 100%. You’ll use Soul Codex books to enhance these. The cost for the books raise each time you enhance. An upgrade is guaranteed but the amount is RNG.
After reaching 100% Soul Binding Rate, you’ll be able to add an additional Stat Line by using 2 Philosopher’s Stones (obtained mainly from Conquest Dungeons).
Important: This extra Stat Line will not transfer over when transferring gear
Sync & Reset
Sync is how you reroll your Stat Lines. Reset is shuffling all of your Stat Lines at once. Full breakdown provided in Chapter 3 - Guide: Ideal Stat Lines.
E - Transfer
Transfer allows you to transfer your gear to another, hopefully upgraded, piece of gear (notice the extra line from the Philosopher’s says it cannot transfer)
Transferring cost “Transfer Stones.” These are obtained by Substance Morphing Transfer Stone Fragments. Other than select shops (such as Season Shop), Transfer Stone Fragments can only be obtained by Substance Morphing Orange/Heroic grade gear. 1 Heroic Gear = 5 Fragments
10 fragments (2 Heroics) = 1 Transfer Stone Accessory
15 fragments (3 Heroics) = 1 Transfer Stone Armor
20 fragments (4 Heroics) = 1 Transfer Stone Weapon
⬅️
⬅️
When Transfering Yellow/Unique gear to Orange/Heroic, 5 additional yellow Transfer Stones are required. When transferring an item of the same grade (Yellow to yellow, orange to orange), 1 Transfer Stone of the same grade is required.
Yellow/Unique Transfer Stones are obtained the same way as Orange/Heroics but be mindful of the Yellow/Unique gear you turn into Transfer Fragments. The x1 - x5 blue number represents how many Fragments you get from that piece. The higher the Item Level, the more Fragments you get.
Important: Be frugal with Orange/Heroic Transfer Stones/Fragments. These are not THAT easy come across, if you save these early on you’ll have a nice supply for later gear. Orange/Heroic gear is MUCH more difficult and expensive to upgrade altogether so be sure to only transfer when you’re ready. I personally recommend not transferring or using the first Heroic Set (Ancient Spirit) and wait for the second set before going Orange/Heroic (Faded Shadow) unless additional changes are made to the game. Currently you can NOT upgrade a Yellow/Unique Transfer stone to an Orange/Heroic one. Transferring and Rolling lines on Yellow/Unique is free compared to Orange/Heroic). You need a LOT of Orange/Heroic Transfer Fragments for the future so just horde them early on.

If this is confusing, just think of the Cards as Gear in most MMOs - you have different types of Gear pieces like Helmet, Chest, Pants…then you have set bonuses for those gear pieces.

Upgrading your Arcana
Farming Transcendence Dungeons will give you Arcana Cards and Training Arcana. You can smash Arcana Cards into the one you equip or feed Training Arcana into it to level it up.
Each level will grant you +1 to a random Skill line you have on the card (or a random Stat by a little if there are no Skills). You can level a Card up to a maximum of level 5.
You can feed whatever Arcana Card you have equipped into a new one if the new one has better lines. However, when you feed it into the new card you will lose a % of the experience you pumped into your current one, the EXP does not transfer over 1:1 (it’s still often worth transferring). You also gain bonus experience for feeding the same type of Arcana Card.
*Global Update: It does not matter what card you feed into another, all of the experience is now the same for Global. You do NOT get bonus experience for feeding the same exact card and card type. All experience is transferred 1:1 (so if you feed a card into another…then feed that fed card into another, all the exp will transfer over)
Crafting Arcana Cards
You can also craft your own Arcana Card via the Transmute section. This allows you to choose all of your lines for most of the Arcana Cards at a cost. This is 100% worth doing and should be done if possible.
Crafting your own Cards will come at the cost of Crystals. Different Arcana Cards cost different Crystals. These Crystals can be Substanced Morphed by consuming Shard versions of the Crystal which are farmed from Transcendence Dungeons - different Dungeons giving different Shards.
It’s important to identify what your biggest value Skills are and which card they come from. You want to prioritize getting your key Active Skills to level 20 ASAP.
Depending on how content is released and your group, I would recommend using or putting together a cheaper Arcana set that may not be exactly be BIS lines but helps you get your Active Skills to reach level 20. Once you do this you can sit on it while you farm more Transcendence to craft your 4-line crafted Card.
Chalice and Scale has the most possible lines therefore it is the most difficult card to get perfect lines on. The other cards roll 4 lines from a pool of 6 meaning the likelihood of getting your ideal lines just from a dungeon drop or a yolo craft are pretty high. If you’re planning on doing a 4-line craft, craft the Chalice first.
Chalice and Scale have the biggest value considering you can roll any Active Skill or Passive Skill. Scale, however, is double the cost (it also won’t be available at launch).

5-Card Setup
There’s some flexibility with the early 5-Card setup. At this stage there are only 2 Set Effects, both being less value than the Pantheon Stats attached to the cards so we prioritize the Pantheon Stats. # PET GENUS
Pet Genus is a horizontal system that gives a lot of power. You have 5 boards - Cogni, Fera, Natura, Varian, Special. You can literally just think of this as Boards 1-5.
Each board will have 9 stats that they can roll called “Slots” (or “Lines” as we refer to them as). Each Line (slot) has its own respective stat pool to roll from. As you level up your “Insight” (the blue bar, commonly referred to as Genus level), you unlock more Lines (slots) and the ability to roll higher grade stats on those Lines (slots).
When you click the yellow Analyze button, you roll random stats with random grades relative to your Insight level for all open Lines. You may lock a Line by clicking on the lock icon to the left of the stat. The more stats you lock, the more expensive the next roll becomes
The goal is to get your Genus level to 10 for all the boards then fish for the best stats for each slot (9 slots, 5 boards).

End-game Stat Value
The following shows the damage efficiency for offensive stats based on current end-game Korea (on an ideal build) which is currently at the start of Season 1 (Start of level 50 and Lava Heart armor introduction). These stats also incorporate support buffs.
Generally speaking, diminishing returns are a thing. This means the values below will be different for Global launch (as well as future KR since more stats are added) considering we will have less stats, therefore less diminishing return impact. There will be a greater discrepancy with some stats for global launch. For example, Front/Back Damage Boost starts at a base value of 0% vs Critical Damage starting at 50%. The first 10% added to both stats favor Front/Back Damage Boost due to diminishing returns.
Also keep in mind certain classes have access to some buffs which diminish the value of certain stats the more you stack them (for example, the Critical Damage Boost stat with Gladiator).
Before we dive into the numbers, quick explanation on…
How Diminishing Returns Work using Double Chance Efficiency Example
What is Double Chance?
A: It gives you a certain chance to deal 2× damage.
If my normal damage is 1, then when Double Chanceprocs, the damage becomes 2.
How much stronger does 1% Double Chance make you?
Imagine that you have a 1% chance for your salary to be doubled.
If your salary is 1,000,000 won, then over 100 paychecks:
1,000,000 won × 100 = 100,000,000 won + 1,000,000 won
Since this represents receiving your salary 100 times, divide the total by 100 to find out how much you receive on average per paycheck.
My normal damage is 1, and when Double Chance procs, it becomes 2.
Double Chance 0% → 1%
How much does my DPS increase?
A: 1.01 → 1% DPS increase
Double Chance 0% → 2%
How much does my DPS increase?
A: 1.02 → 2% DPS increase
Double Chance 0% → 50%
How much does my DPS increase?
A: 1.5 → 50% DPS increase
Double Chance 1% → 2%
How much does my DPS increase?
A: 0.99%
At 1% Double Chance: 101 total damage At 2% Double Chance: 102 total damage
So going from 101 → 102 is approximately a 0.99% actual DPS increase.
Practical Calculation
At 80% Double Chance, how efficient is another +1% Double Chance?
Assumptions:
Double Chance Resistance: 30% 보빛 + 대징 support buffs: +12% Double Chance
80% Double Chance → 62% Final Double Chance
Now, how much DPS do you gain going from:
62% → 63% Double Chance?
If your base damage is 1 and you attack 100 times:
63 attacks deal 2 damage from Double Chance.
37 attacks deal 1 damage.
Therefore:
62% Double Chance = 162 total damage 63% Double Chance = 163 total damage
Answer: 0.617% DPS increase
So:
+1% Double Chance = approximately +0.617% actual DPS at this point.

Multi-Hit
KR
A % chance for your Attack to land 1-4 extra hits.
Each hit is 5% of the Damage
When Multi-hit rate is in the 36% ~ 40% range (current KR endgame range) every 1% of hit is roughly ≈ to 0.33% damage increase
Global
Each hit is 2% of the Damage (needs more testing, rumored to be higher on bosses)

This stat is still being studied and its exact value and placement in the equation is still not 100%. More testing needs to be done to establish whether it is with or outside Attack Bonus.
Power Shards
Power Shards are consumables you equip that enhance your damage by a % value. Power Shards have slight diminishing returns from the PVE Attack bucket. The damage increase efficiency comes out to:
(Regular) Power Shard = 4.65% more damage
(Yellow) Superior Power Shard = 8.9% more damage (4.05% more than Regulars)
Accuracy & Block/Parry
Accuracy ensures your attack lands as a clean hit negating a Block/Parry 1:1. If your Accuracy covers the exact amount of Block a target has, you will land 100% of your attacks.
There are diminishing returns to this stat
The average value of 100 Accuracy is roughly ≈ 6% ~ 7%
If my Target has 2,000 Block and my Accuracy is 1,000 then he has a 60% ~ 70% chance to Parry
Parry is capped at an 80% chance and a difference of 1,200 is required to reach it
This allows us to calculate the 6% ~ 7% per 100 (6.66…%*1,200 = 80%)
The graph below represents how the diminishing returns work for both Crit/Crit Resistance and Accuracy/Block
Critical Hit & Critical Hit Resist
Critical Hit & Critical Resist use the same formula as Accuracy & Block/Parry which includes the 80% cap.
Refer to the graph and Accuracy explanation above
Skill passives are Additive so if your Critical Hit is 70% and you take a Skill passive that increases your Critical Hit by 30%, your Critical Hit % will be 80%. If you are hitting 80% Crit on a Skill you do not need to take a Crit increasing Specialty Perk
Defense & Penetration
Defense reduces Damage taken by a %. Penetration subtracts directly from Defense. Penetration is overall a very low value stat due to how Defense works.
In PVE, Damage will be reduced by 10% of your Defense value
In PVP, Damage will be reduced by 1% of your Defense value
Example Scenario:
Damage dealt is 500 with 100 penetration against a target with 200 defense
The Defense after Penetration is 100 (200 Defense - 100 Penetration)
10% of 100 = 10
500 - 10 = 490 Damage after Defense and Penetration calculations (499 for PVP)
Damage Tolerance & Endurance
Damage Tolerance and Endurance are both highly effective Defensive stats. Damage Tolerance directly reduces Damage Boost against you while Endurance reduces your final damage taken by half.
Damage Tolerance
All Damage Tolerance is a direct counter to its respective Damage Boost
In PVE Damage Tolerance, Boss Damage Tolerance, and PVE Damage Tolerance are the only ones of value considering Bosses do have Damage Boost (impossible to measure exactly how much due to damage being random)
Bosses do not have Front/Back Damage Boost, Weapon Damage Boost, Critical Hit, or Multi-hit meaning the respective Tolerance and Resistance against those stats are worthless
Endurance
Endurance is only negated by Endurance Penetration. There is no Endurance Penetration from Bosses so whatever the % you see in your stat sheet will be the % chance you have to take 50% damage.
Additional Notes: Combat Speed is a given S-tier individual stat that you stack as much as you can. This, however, is directly impacted by ping and FPS. When tested, a difference of 150-200 ping equated to roughly ≈ 15% ~ 20% damage. Contrary to popular belief, there were no major differences between classes. Templar, Brawler, and Assassin were the three classes tested and were roughly similar. Spiritmaster may be an outlier due to the passive pet damage.
Damage Formula
English:
[[[((Pure Attack × Weapon Damage Boost) × Multi-Hit × Power Shard) + Attack Bonus) × Attack Increase % + PvE Attack Power + Boss Attack Power + Species Attack Power + Front/Back Attack Power] × Skill Coefficient] + Skill Additional Damage − [(Defense − Penetration) × 0.1]] × (Damage Boost + PvE Damage Boost + Boss Damage Boost + Species Damage Boost) × Critical Damage Boost × Double × Front/Back Damage Boost × Skill Specialty Enhancement = Final Damage

Math heads and theory crafters, here’s your baseline…
Important - What is “Pure Attack”?
“Pure Attack” is not an official stat in the game. It’s a label created to categorize the Attack that gets directly multiplied by Weapon Damage Boost. There are a lot of different types of “Attack” stats in-game but they are spread across the equation.
You can test the above by calculating the total Attack you get from your Weapon and removing it along with your Weapon Damage Boost % and see what the “Attack” value shows under your Stat Info > Main Stats page. The “Attack” value on here does not consider Weapon Damage Boost%
The stats that fall under “Pure Attack” is as follows:
Weapon Attack
Guard Attack
Accessories Attack
Attack Stat Lines
Manastone/Soulstone Attack
Closet
Further Detail
Outside of “Pure Attack” the rest of the “Attacks” are added together starting from PVE Attack in the equation above. Because this added after Attack Increase the value is less than Pure Attack. When it comes to the flat “Attack” stats the value order is:
Tier 1
Attack (Pure Attack)
Max Attack (Pure Attack)
Attack Bonus (within Attack Increase)
Tier 2
PVE Attack
Boss Attack
Tier 3 (Conditional)
Front/Back Attack
Species Attack
Critical Attack
Tier 3 stats are the worst considering they do not fall under “Pure Attack” and only get added to PVE/Boss Attack upon condition. Species being specific to mob type.
